ALA-LC Romanization Tables


Romanization Landscape and Guidelines

Romanization Landscape statement is now available.

Procedural Guidelines for Proposed New or Revised Romanization Tables have been approved and are now available.

  • NEW Complete list of languages and scripts for the Script Expansion Project

    The Library of Congress announced the expansion of non-Latin script input in bibliographic and authority records on September 18. Since then, the Library has been working closely with OCLC to ensure that LC records distributed to OCLC are correctly rendered in its original script including both bibliographic and authority records. While OCLC is still conducting tests in their systems, the Library of Congress has moved forward with inputting additional scripts into our catalog records. We are pleased to share that we have started adding scripts in both bibliographic and authority records as indicated in the table at the link.

    Additionally, the expansion includes the following areas that were not part of the original announcement.

    • Armenian, Thai, and Mongolian input in authority records
    • The extended Cyrillic character set
    • All CJK Unicode characters
    • Indigenous North American languages
  • Character Sets Used in Romanization
  • Index to Languages  (Revised February 2012)

The 1997 edition of the tables is also available for purchase in book form from the Cataloging Distribution Service while supplies last.
