= BGN/PCGN coverage

The BGN/PCGN romanization systems are joint standards of the United
States Board on Geographic Names (BGN) and the Permanent Committee on
Geographical Names for British Official Use (PCGN). The Interscript
corpus implements 55 BGN/PCGN maps across 41 language codes. Four of
those codes, namely `deu`, `fao`, `isl`, and `sme`, are Roman-script
spelling conventions rather than conversions from another script.

== Reference tables without a map in the corpus

for the following systems, and the corpus does not yet provide a map
for them.

The collection also contains the 2012 revision of the Kurdish system,
while the corpus implements the 2007 edition. The corpus represents
Hebrew through the ICAO 9303 map and Khmer through a neural model;
neither substitutes for a BGN/PCGN rule map.

== Neural model coverage

The deterministic map and the neural model are independent products.
Neural models are published for Hebrew, Khmer, Thai, Persian, Arabic,
and Urdu. No neural model is published for Burmese, Dzongkha,
Inuktitut, Lao, or Shan, because no parallel corpus of source-script
names with romanized equivalents is available for training in those
languages.
