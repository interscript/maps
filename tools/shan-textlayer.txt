Notes  
1. Some symbols in the Shan script are attached above, below, or beside a 
consonant. Where such symbols are listed in this table, the symbol “◌” is 
used to represent the consonant.  
2. Absence of a vowel symbol in the script is represented by a in 
romanization, with the following exceptions:  
(a) When a different vowel is indicated by its appropriate symbol (see 
Vowels);

word division as in the original language.
